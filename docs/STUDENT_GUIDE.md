# ITSA Platform: Student User Guide

## 1. Welcome to ITSA
The **Information Technology Students' Association (ITSA) Platform** is your centralized hub for campus technology events, workshops, hackathons, verified certificates, gamification points, and community discussions.

---

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Registration (`/register`)
1. Click **Join ITSA** or navigate to `/register`.
2. Enter your Full Name, College Roll Number / Student ID (e.g. `CS2023001`), College Email, Department, and Year of Study.
3. Choose a secure password (minimum 8 characters).
4. Click **Register**. Your student account and profile are created immediately.

### 2.2 Logging In (`/login`)
1. Enter your college email and password.
2. Check **Keep me logged in** if using your personal computer.
3. You will be redirected directly to your **Student Dashboard**.

---

## 3. Student Features & Navigation

### 3.1 Student Dashboard (`/student/dashboard`)
- **Metric Cards**: View your ITSA Points balance, total attended events, earned certificates, and AI Engagement Index.
- **My Registered Events**: Quick access to your confirmed upcoming events with dates, times, and venue rooms.
- **AI Event Recommendations**: 3 personalized events recommended based on your department, academic year, and interests.

### 3.2 Event Directory & Registration (`/events`)
- Filter events by Category (Technical, Workshop, Competition, Seminar) or search by keyword.
- Open any event to view full descriptions, speakers, schedule, and venue details.
- Click **Register for Event**. If registration is open and within capacity, your confirmation is instant, and your digital ticket is generated immediately.

### 3.3 My Tickets with QR Code (`/student/tickets`)
- Every confirmed registration generates a unique ticket containing an encrypted QR code (`ITSA-TKT-...`).
- At the venue entrance, open `/student/tickets` and present the QR code to the coordinator for scanning.

### 3.4 Attendance & Certificates (`/student/attendance` & `/student/certificates`)
- **Attendance Log**: View all events where your attendance was verified by a coordinator.
- **Official Certificates**: Once marked `PRESENT`, your verified completion certificate is generated instantly.
- **Download PDF**: Download high-resolution, print-ready PDF certificates with unique verification codes and digital signatures.
- **Public Verification**: Anyone (recruiters, faculty) can verify your certificate authenticity at `/certificates/verify/<code_id>`.

### 3.5 ITSA Community Feed (`/feed`)
- Share project updates, hackathon achievements, and questions.
- React with 5 expressive sentiments (Like, Love, Celebrate, Insightful, Support).
- Engage in threaded comments and reply to peers.
- Tag topics using `#hashtags` and mention friends using `@name`.

### 3.6 ITSA AI Assistant (`/student/chatbot`)
- Ask questions about event rules, team sizes, prerequisite tools, registration deadlines, and ITSA policies.
- Powered by Google Gemini 2.0 Flash for instant, helpful, and friendly academic guidance.

### 3.7 Leaderboard & Points (`/leaderboard`)
- Earn ITSA Points for active engagement:
  - **+10 pts** for attending an event
  - **+3 pts** for registering for an event
  - **+5 pts** for submitting event feedback
  - **+2 pts** for creating community posts
  - **+1 pt** for constructive comments
- Climb the student leaderboard and unlock engagement badges.
